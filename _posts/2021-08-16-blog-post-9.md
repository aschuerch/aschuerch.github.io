In his recent publication [Recovering Escherichia coli Plasmids in the Absence of Long-Read Sequencing Data ](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-2607/9/8/1613),

![Euler diagram of tools to predict the plasmidome of bacteria](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-2607/9/8/1613)
