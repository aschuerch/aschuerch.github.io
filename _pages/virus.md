My work in viral genomics and metagenomics has revolved around the use of next-generation sequencing to recover full-length viral genomes from complex metagenomic datasets, enabling the discovery and characterization of novel and uncultivated viruses. I also worked on the detection of respiratory viruses and identified drug-resistant hepatitis C virus variants in treatment-naïve patients, providing critical insights for clinical diagnostics and personalized medicine.
