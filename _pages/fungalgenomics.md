Candida infections (candidiasis) are a major global health threat, especially in immunocompromised patients. The genomics of fungal pathogens, especially Candida species, focuses on using high-throughput DNA sequencing and computational analysis to understand how these fungi can transmit, adapt to hosts, and develop drug resistance.
